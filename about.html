About · Since 1975

Gainesville’s shop, since 1975.

Waiting on your vehicle doesn’t have to mean waiting around. That’s how we’ve looked after Gainesville since 1975.

Our story

Customers and their carscome first.

Dave Mays Automotive has been fixing Gainesville’s cars since 1975. We believe no job is too big: classic cars, day-to-day vehicles and fleet vehicles all roll through our bays.

What hasn’t changed is how we do it. We tell you straight what your car needs, give you options that fit your budget, and stand behind our work with a 2-year/20,000-mile warranty on most parts and labor.

Some customers have been coming for decades. One family has brought all six of their cars to us over almost 30 years. Another drives up from St. Petersburg.
