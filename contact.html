FAQ

Quick answers.

What are your hours?

Monday to Thursday 7:30 AM to 5:30 PM and Friday 7:30 AM to 5:00 PM. We’re closed Saturday and Sunday. Holiday hours may vary, so call ahead around holidays.

Where are you?

2905 NE 19th Dr, Gainesville, FL 32609. Look for the red-and-white diamond sign out front.

Do you offer a warranty?

Yes. Most of our parts and labor carry a 2-year/20,000-mile warranty. Ask us what’s covered on your specific repair.

Is there a loaner car?

We have a loaner vehicle. Ask about it when you call to set up your repair.

What vehicles do you work on?

Classic cars, day-to-day vehicles and fleet vehicles.

Are your technicians certified?

Yes, our technicians are ASE certified.

Can I wait while you work?

Yes. Our comfortable lobby gives you a clean, relaxed space while we take care of your car.
